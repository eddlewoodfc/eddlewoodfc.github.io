Getting here & parking

Eddlewood Park

Queens Drive, Hamilton ML3 7ST

There is parking at the ground for around 30–40 cars. On matchdays a further small car park on the other side of the park can also be used — it's a short walk to the ground.

Hamilton is well served by bus and rail; use the directions link to plan your journey.
